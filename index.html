A night audiences remember

Southern rock with the room turned up.

Lightning in the Air faithfully recreates the signature blend of Southern rock, country, blues, and jazz that made The Marshall Tucker Band legendary.

From “Can’t You See” and “Heard It in a Love Song” to “Fire on the Mountain,” “Searchin’ for a Rainbow,” and deeper fan favorites, the show is built for sing-alongs, musicianship, and a crowd that wants one more song.
