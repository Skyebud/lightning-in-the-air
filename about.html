The band

Keeping the music alive.

Experienced musicians, authentic arrangements, and the unmistakable blend of Southern rock, country, blues, and jazz.

Alabama's Marshall Tucker tribute

A full concert experience.

Lightning in the Air is a high-energy Marshall Tucker Band tribute based in Alabama, dedicated to preserving the sound and spirit of one of Southern rock’s most iconic bands.

Featuring experienced musicians and a passion for authentic performance, the band faithfully recreates the classic hits that helped define a generation, including “Can’t You See,” “Heard It in a Love Song,” “Fire on the Mountain,” and many more.

Whether performing at festivals, theaters, casinos, clubs, concert series, or community events, the band brings fans an unforgettable journey through the golden era of Southern rock.
